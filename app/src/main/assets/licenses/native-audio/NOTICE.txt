Native audio components in this Android / Android TV build

BitChord's native LoopMix renderer and JNI bridge retain GPL-3.0-or-later.
Shared LoopMix, Orchard-derived analysis and transition policies retain their
AGPL-3.0-or-later attribution. The complete GPL and AGPL texts are included here.
SOURCE-ATTRIBUTION.txt preserves the original inspected source headers.
UPSTREAM-AutoMix-NOTICE.md is the original upstream Web attribution; its Web
implementation descriptions do not assert native behavior or source availability
for this unpublished integration.

Signalsmith Stretch 1.3.2 and Signalsmith Linear 0.6.6 are header-only MIT
components compiled into libbitchord_loopmix.so. Both original MIT texts are here.

Oboe 1.11.0 (Apache-2.0) is statically linked into libbitchord_exclusive.so.
libusb 1.0.27 (LGPL-2.1-or-later) is statically linked into libbitchord_usb.so.
Both archives are SHA-256 pinned by app/src/main/cpp/CMakeLists.txt. Their
original license texts and inspected attribution are included here.

The AAudio/USB output JNI and listed Kotlin bit-perfect files adapt Mornye,
Copyright 2026 zarzet, and retain LGPL-3.0-only headers. The LGPL3 text included
here was retrieved from Mornye LICENSE at the revision in provenance.json.
That retrieval revision identifies only the license text: the exact historical
Mornye code revision used by the adaptation remains unresolved.

The DST decoder is LGPL-2.1-or-later. Its exact COPYING and modification/provenance
notice are included separately. Reverse engineering to debug modifications to
the LGPL-covered components is permitted.
